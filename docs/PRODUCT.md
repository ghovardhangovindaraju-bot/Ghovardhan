# WAC Console product definition

## Product position
WAC Console is a cloud ERP for UK fuel importers, distributors, blenders and stockholders serving mid-sized operations that need a practical replacement for fragmented spreadsheets and paper approval chains.

The product is designed around the operating flow:

- procurement and cargo intake
- stock and WAC valuation
- sales pricing and transport scheduling
- invoicing and AP automation
- bank reconciliation and period close
- VAT and compliance reporting

The focus is not routing or telematics. The focus is the commercial and financial engine behind the fuel business.

## Core customer problem
Fuel businesses manage a mix of volatile purchase pricing, margin pressure, stock variance and regulatory obligations. The same operation often runs across spreadsheets, freight emails, bank statements and accounting tools.

That creates:

- slow purchase approvals
- opaque landed cost calculations
- inconsistent stock valuation
- missed supplier invoice controls
- delayed month-end close
- weak compliance evidence

WAC Console brings these into one operating system with a clear audit trail.

## Product principles
1. Every business event is traceable and auditable.
2. All costing maths sits in a dedicated engine and is pure.
3. WAC is rebuilt from events and not maintained manually.
4. Approval workflows are enforced within the ERP.
5. Finance and operations must share the same source of truth.
6. The system must support UK fuel compliance and period-close discipline.

## Solution scope
### Procurement and supply
- supplier contracts with tolerance and pricing rules
- cargo contracts with delivery terms and FX
- vessel timeline and BL variance tracking
- documents checklist and shared logistics link
- supplier approval steps before cargo release

### Stock and costing
- purchase receipts and stock movements
- WAC, landed cost and margin calculation
- product valuation by stock location and batch
- stock and cost-of-sales split after provisional settlement

### Sales and transport
- price sheet built from WAC or replacement cost
- minimum-margin guard
- rep publish and approval flow
- tanker and haulier scheduling
- unallocated order backlog

### Finance
- AP invoice matching and workflow approval
- bank reconciliation and statements
- GL journals and period reporting
- invoice pipeline and receivables
- VAT return and close checklist

### Overview
- team messages per record and per channel
- margin analysis by rep
- exception reporting for variances and approval states

## Target market
Mid-sized UK fuel importers, distributors, blenders and stockholders with:

- annual volumes that justify a dedicated commercial system
- multi-site or multi-product operations
- regulated reporting needs
- enough complexity to outgrow spreadsheets

## Commercial differentiation
The product is differentiated by being built for fuel rather than for generic manufacturing or wholesale distribution. That means it understands:

- landed cost per litre
- density and temperature adjustments
- outturn variance and tolerances
- demurrage and laytime
- duty and VAT treatment
- price bands and minimum margin controls

## Automation strategy
The ERP will automate the repetitive and high-risk parts of the business where possible:

- AP invoice capture and matching
- approval routing based on threshold and supplier risk
- provisional price settlement and stock/cost-of-sales split
- bank reconciliation suggestions
- month-end close checklists and period lock
- VAT checks before submission

This reduces manual work while improving control and audit quality.
